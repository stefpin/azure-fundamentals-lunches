Lunch Tutor: privacy and AI use

An AI study buddy for this course, now in a private pilot. Here is what it does, who can use it and what happens to your questions.

What Lunch Tutor is · Pilot status · How to use it · Using AI responsibly · Privacy · For IT administrators · Not an official Microsoft service

What Lunch Tutor is

Lunch Tutor is an AI tutor for this course. Open it on any lunch or lab page and ask about what you are reading or building: it knows that lesson and its lab, and it answers in the language of the page. It gives hints first, so you still do the thinking — ask for the full answer if you are still stuck. It uses Azure OpenAI (GPT-5 mini).

Pilot status

Lunch Tutor is in a private pilot. It is open to people from organizations whose IT team has approved it, and to personal Microsoft accounts on the invite list.

All learners share one limit of 100 questions a day. When they are used up, the tutor reopens at 07:00 WIB (00:00 UTC).

How to use it

  1. Open a lunch or a lab and select “Ask the tutor” at the bottom right of the page.
  2. Select “Sign in with Microsoft”: you go to Microsoft's sign-in page and come straight back to the same page. Use your work or school account, or an invited personal account.
  3. Type your question (up to 1,000 characters) and select Ask. You get a hint first; ask for the full answer if you are still stuck.
  4. The conversation stays on the page only until you leave or reload it.

Using AI responsibly

  • AI answers can be wrong. Check important details in Microsoft Learn before you rely on them.
  • Don't paste secrets — passwords, keys, connection strings or tokens — or personal data into your questions.
  • The tutor helps you learn; it doesn't replace the readings, the labs or your own judgement.

Privacy

  • The tutor stores an anonymous ID derived from your account — not your name or email — plus your questions and its answers for 30 days. Then they are deleted automatically.
  • You can delete them at any time with “Delete my tutor history” in the tutor panel.
  • The data is stored in Azure in the Indonesia Central region. The AI model runs in a Global deployment, so your questions may be processed in any Azure region where the model runs.
  • Nothing you send is used to train AI models.
  • Your course progress stays in your browser. With each question the panel sends only the numbers of the lunches you have finished, so the tutor knows what you have already learned.
  • Signing in keeps a Microsoft sign-in token in this browser. “Sign out” in the panel removes it from this site; it doesn't sign you out of Microsoft.

For IT administrators

The app is called “Lunch Tutor”. It is registered in the course author's lab directory, so the consent screen shows it as “unverified”.

It asks only for sign-in, basic profile, offline access and its own Tutor.Ask permission — no access to mail, files or calendars.

An administrator with the Cloud Application Administrator or Application Administrator role can approve it once for the whole organization with this link (replace {your-tenant-id} with your directory (tenant) ID):

https://login.microsoftonline.com/{your-tenant-id}/adminconsent?client_id=0cbc5d81-ada6-436e-b8f1-abcc562c62a7

  • To limit it to a pilot group, set “Assignment required” to Yes in Enterprise applications › Lunch Tutor › Properties, then assign that group.
  • You can remove it at any time: delete “Lunch Tutor” under Enterprise applications.
  • Approval alone doesn't open the tutor: it only accepts organizations on its allow-list. To ask about joining the pilot, email stefanus.pinarta@gmail.com.
