FAQ

Before you grab your lunch

Do I need an Azure subscription?

Not for the readings, demos and quizzes. For the labs, yes — Episode 01 walks you through the options (a free account, a pay-as-you-go subscription or a sandbox your employer provides) and sets up a budget alert before you create anything.

How much will the labs cost?

Every lab shows a cost band: 💲 is free or close to free, 💲💲 means small charges while resources run, 💲💲💲 means noticeable charges. Every lab ends with a one-command clean-up, and the lab page reminds you if you forget. Prices vary by region, so the labs never quote numbers — use the Azure pricing calculator.

Does this prepare me for AZ-900?

Season 1 (Episodes 01–21) covers the AZ-900 skill areas; every episode shows which skill area it belongs to. Season 2 goes deliberately beyond AZ-900 into what you need on the job: hub-and-spoke networks, Kubernetes, KQL and governance at scale.

Is this official Microsoft training?

No. It is an independent, personal learning project, not endorsed by Microsoft. Azure changes often, so every episode links to Microsoft Learn for the details — and every page shows when it was last validated.

Who wrote this course?

Stefanus Pinarta, a Jakarta-based solutions engineer with more than twenty years in enterprise IT who has worked inside all three major cloud providers. See “About the author” below; for any further inquiries, email stefanus.pinarta@gmail.com.

Is there an Indonesian version?

Yes. Every episode, lab, demo and badge is available in Bahasa Indonesia: use the language switch at the top of any page. Your progress is shared between both languages.

Where is my progress stored?

Only in this browser (local storage). Nothing about you is sent anywhere. In My lunchbox you can export your progress to a file and import it on another device.

I only have 30 minutes today. Now what?

Start the lunch timer, do the scenario and reading, and come back for the demo and quiz tomorrow. The timer and every checkbox remember where you were.
